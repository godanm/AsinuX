Kazhutha for Beginners: Follow Suit and Cut

Read the full rules · All guides

Kazhutha on Tricksy is a four-player trick-taking game. Your goal is to empty your hand. The last player with cards is the Donkey and is eliminated from the tournament.

Example 1: Everybody follows suit

Leela leads the four of hearts. Ravi plays the jack, Meera plays the six and Arun plays the king. All four cards are hearts, so the cards are discarded and Arun leads next.

Example 2: The first cut ends the trick

Leela leads the four of hearts again and Ravi plays the jack. Meera has no hearts and plays a spade. Ravi takes the three cards and leads next. Arun keeps his card because the cut ended the trick before his turn.

Watch who is running out of a suit

When a player cuts, remember which suit they were unable to follow. Leading that suit again can end another trick early. If you hold a high card in the lead suit, a cut can send the whole pile back to your hand.
