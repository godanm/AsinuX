Kazhutha for Beginners: Follow Suit and Cut

Read the full rules · All guides

Kazhutha begins with four seats and a standard 52-card deck, so each player starts with 13 cards. A trick is one sequence of plays around the table. Your aim is to empty your hand and escape the round. The last player holding cards becomes the Donkey and is eliminated.

Read the first card before choosing yours

The player dealt the ace of spades leads the opening trick and may lead any card. Its suit becomes the lead suit. If you have that suit, choose a card of that suit. If you have none, play any card to cut. Within the lead suit, twos rank lowest and aces highest. There is no trump suit.

Example 1: Everybody follows suit

Leela leads the four of hearts. Ravi plays the jack of hearts, Meera plays the six of hearts, and Arun plays the king of hearts. All four cards are discarded. Arun played the highest heart and leads the next trick if he still has cards. If that king was his last card, the lead passes to the next player in rotation who still has cards.

Example 2: The first cut ends the trick

In a separate example, Leela leads the four of hearts and Ravi plays the jack of hearts. Meera has no hearts and plays the ace of spades. Ravi takes all three cards and leads next. The ace of spades triggers the cut, and the jack remains the highest heart. Arun keeps his card because the trick ended before his turn.

Check whether your last card really left your hand

Suppose Ravi's jack was his last card in the second example. The cut puts the pile into his hand, so he stays in the round. If Meera's ace was her last card, she escapes because the pile goes to Ravi. Wait for the trick to resolve before counting a player out.

Use information from the current trick

  • Follow under a higher card when it helps. If the jack of hearts is already down and you hold the six and king of hearts, the six leaves someone else ahead if a later player cuts. Playing the king puts you at risk of taking the pile.
  • Consider shedding a high card when you cut. A king in another suit may be hard to get rid of later. When you are out of the lead suit, the cut lets you send that king into the pile without competing for the lead.
  • Watch for void suits. A cut shows that the cutter had none of the lead suit at that moment. Recheck that inference after they pick up a pile or receive a new deal.

From one round to the tournament

The first player to escape earns one round score point. Each round eliminates one player. The remaining players receive a fresh deal, and the last player who avoids elimination wins the tournament. The next deal again uses the ace-of-spades holder to pick the opener. If the ace is among undealt cards, the first active seat leads.
