Tricksy Blackjack: Hit, Stand or Double Down

Read the full rules · All guides

Tricksy Blackjack pairs you with a computer dealer and uses virtual chips. Your choices are Hit, Stand and, on an eligible two-card hand, Double Down.

Try a hard and a soft total

Ten and seven form a hard 17. Drawing a five takes that hand to 22. An ace and a six form a soft 17. Drawing the same five gives 12 because the ace can count as 1. Check how the ace is counted before deciding whether another card can bust the hand.

Read the dealer’s visible card

The dealer keeps drawing below 17 and stands on 17 or higher. If you stand on 18 and the dealer has 16, the dealer must draw. A two ties your 18, a three beats it and a six makes the dealer bust.

Know when your turn ends

Hit leaves you free to act again if the hand continues. Stand sends play to the dealer. Double Down increases the virtual stake, draws exactly one card and ends your turn. A pair stays in one hand in this version.
