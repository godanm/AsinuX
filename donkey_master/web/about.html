What is Tricksy?

Tricksy is a free card game app that brings together eight games under one roof. Whether you're waiting for a friend or killing time, you can jump into a game in seconds — no sign-up, no password, no fees.

Play Blackjack against the dealer or Bluff against three bots. Kazhutha, Game 28 and Teen Patti also start with bots. Rummy, Tambola and Wild Card use public matchmaking. Chips and points are virtual and have no cash value.
