🃏 Donkey (Kazhutha)

Trick-taking · 4 players · Escape before the Donkey tag finds you

Play Now — Free

How to Play

🏃 Objective

Empty your hand before everyone else. The last player still holding cards is eliminated as the Donkey.

  • 4 players, 13 cards each
  • Survive rounds to win the tournament
  • Last player standing wins

🃏 Setup

Cards are dealt evenly from a standard 52-card deck. The player holding the Ace of Spades leads first.

  • Standard deck, no jokers
  • Ace of Spades always leads round 1
  • Cards are sorted by suit in your hand

🔄 Playing a Trick

The leader plays any card — that suit is the lead suit. Everyone else must follow the same suit if they can.

  • Must follow the lead suit if you have it
  • Highest lead-suit card wins the trick
  • Winner leads the next trick

✂️ The Cut

If you have no cards of the lead suit, play any card. This is a cut — the trick ends immediately.

  • The player with the highest lead-suit card picks up all played cards
  • The pickup player leads the next trick
  • Use cuts to force dangerous high cards onto opponents

🏆 Escaping & Scoring

Play your last card to escape the round. Points are awarded for order of escape.

  • 1st to escape: +100 pts
  • 2nd to escape: +60 pts
  • 3rd to escape: +30 pts
  • Donkey (last player): −50 pts, eliminated
