🃏 Blackjack

Beat the dealer · 1 player vs house · For entertainment only — chip-based, no real money

Play Now — Free

How to Play

🏆 Objective

Beat the dealer by getting a hand value closer to 21 without going over. You play against the house — not other players.

  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer
  • Go over 21 and you "bust" — instant loss
  • Dealer busts? You win automatically
  • Equal totals = push (bet returned)

🃏 Card Values

Number cards are worth their face value. Face cards are worth 10. Aces are flexible.

  • 2–10 = face value
  • Jack, Queen, King = 10
  • Ace = 11 or 1 — whichever keeps you under 21
  • Ace + any 10-value card = Blackjack (best hand!)

🎯 Your Actions

After the deal you have three moves. Choose wisely based on your total and the dealer's visible card.

  • Hit — draw another card
  • Stand — keep your hand and end your turn
  • Double Down — double your bet, draw exactly one card, then stand (only on first two cards)

🤖 Dealer Rules

The dealer follows fixed rules — no strategy, no choices. One of the dealer's two cards is hidden until your turn ends.

  • Dealer always hits until reaching 17 or more
  • Dealer stands on 17 and above
  • Hidden card is revealed after you stand or bust

💰 Payouts

Your winnings depend on how you won. Blackjack pays a bonus over a regular win.

  • Regular win → +1× your bet
  • Blackjack (Ace + 10-value) → +1.5× your bet
  • Push (tie) → bet returned, no gain or loss
  • Bust or lower total → lose your bet
