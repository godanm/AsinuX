How to Play

🏆 Objective

Each player is dealt 7 cards. On your turn, play a card that matches the top of the discard pile by color or by number/type. The first player to empty their hand wins.

  • 2, 4, or 6 seats, standard Wild Card deck (108 cards)
  • 4 colors: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow — plus Wild cards
  • Match by color, number, or card type to play
  • Special cards change the direction, skip turns, or force draws
🟥 Red 🟦 Blue 🟩 Green 🟨 Yellow ★ Wild

🎴 Playing a Card

The top card of the discard pile sets the current color and type. You must play a card that matches on at least one of these — or play a Wild card.

  • Same color — always playable regardless of number or type
  • Same number or type — always playable regardless of color (e.g. any 7 on a 7, any Skip on a Skip)
  • Wild card — playable any time; you choose the new color after playing
  • If you cannot or choose not to play, draw one card from the pile. Drawing passes the turn, even if you draw a playable card.

⚡ Special Cards

Action cards have immediate effects on the next player — or on the whole table.

  • Skip — the next player in turn order loses their turn entirely
  • Reverse — the direction of play flips (clockwise ↔ counter-clockwise). At a two-seat table, the other seat still plays next.
  • Draw Two — the next player must draw 2 cards and loses their turn
  • Wild — playable any time; you declare the new color for the next play
  • Wild Draw Four — the next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn, and you declare the new color. Playable regardless of the current color.

🔢 Winning

The first player to play their last card wins the round immediately. There is no scoring — the goal is simply to empty your hand before anyone else.

  • Playing your last card ends the game instantly
  • The winner scores points in the app — all other players score 0

Strategy & Tips

🧠 Winning at Wild Card

Wild Card rewards timing and attention. Knowing when to play action cards — not just that you can — is what separates good players from great ones.

  • Save action cards for when they hurt most. A Skip or Draw Two played when an opponent is down to 1 card is far more valuable than burning it early.
  • Control the color. After playing a Wild, pick the color you hold the most of. You force opponents to either match or draw, while you cruise through your hand.
  • Watch card counts. The player with the fewest cards is your most dangerous opponent — target them with Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two.
  • Don't burn Wild Draw Four early. It's your most powerful card. Save it as a last-card play or to punish an opponent who's about to win.

Choose between color and number

On a blue seven, a red seven matches by number and a blue Skip matches by color. A red Skip matches neither. Playing the blue Skip skips the next seat if the game continues. Drawing one card instead ends your turn.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Wild Draw Four on any card?

Yes. Tricksy permits Wild Draw Four on any discard. Choose the next color. If you still have cards, the next player draws four and is skipped.

What happens if the draw pile runs out?

The discard pile (except the top card) is shuffled and becomes the new draw pile. The game continues without interruption.

Can I stack Draw Two cards?

No — stacking is not used on Tricksy. When a Draw Two is played, the next player draws 2 and loses their turn, regardless of whether they hold a Draw Two themselves.

What if the first card flipped is a Wild?

Tricksy removes Wilds from the opening draw until it finds a non-Wild card to start the discard pile.

Is Wild Card gambling?

On Tricksy, no. All points are virtual scores with no monetary value. You cannot buy, sell, or exchange anything. Wild Card on Tricksy is purely for entertainment.
