🃏 Game 28

Trick-taking · 4 players · 2 teams · Classic South Indian card game

Play Now — Free

How to Play

🏆 Objective

Win 6 game points (or the target) before the other team. Teams bid on tricks and must score at least their bid to earn points.

  • 4 players in 2 teams (A & B)
  • Seats 0 & 2 form Team A; seats 1 & 3 form Team B
  • First team to reach the target wins
  • A round = one full set of 8 tricks

🃏 Cards & Points

Only 8 ranks are used (J, 9, A, 10, K, Q, 8, 7). Card points are counted when you win a trick.

  • Jack = 3 points
  • Nine = 2 points
  • Ace = 1 point, Ten = 1 point
  • K, Q, 8, 7 = 0 points
  • Total points in the deck = 28

🎯 Bidding

Each player gets 4 cards to bid on. Bid how many card points your team will win. Highest bidder wins; remaining 4 cards are dealt after.

  • Bid from 14 (minimum) up to 28
  • First bidder must open — cannot pass
  • Partner rule: must bid 20+ to outbid your own partner
  • Bid winner secretly chooses a trump suit
  • Bidding 28 = Thani — instant bid win, highest stakes

🔄 Playing Tricks

Follow the lead suit if you have it. The highest card of the lead suit wins — unless trump is revealed.

  • Trump beats any non-trump card
  • Bid winner cannot voluntarily lead trump
  • To reveal trump: be void in lead suit, then ask for trump
  • Once trump is revealed, it applies for the rest of the round

📊 Scoring

After all 8 tricks, compare the bid team's card points against their bid to award game points.

  • Bid met: +1 game point (bid ≥ 20: +2 pts)
  • All 28 points won — Thani!: +3 game points
  • Thani declared but failed: opposing team gets 3 game points
  • Bid failed: defending team gets the game points
  • First team to the target score wins the game!
